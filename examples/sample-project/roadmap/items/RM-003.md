# RM-003 — Invitation reminder emails

## User story

As a workspace admin, I want pending invitations to receive a reminder, so that teammates do not miss their invitation.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A reminder is sent three days before expiration.
- [ ] Accepted, revoked, and expired invitations receive no reminder.
- [ ] Administrators can see when a reminder was sent.
